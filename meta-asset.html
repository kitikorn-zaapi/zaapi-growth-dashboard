📖 Reference · what do the angles, metrics, and AI verdicts mean?

Angles

Value Bomb (VB)
Leads with the outcome or pain — "stop losing customers to slow replies" → Zaapi fixes it. Works when the audience feels the pain acutely.
Testimonial (TS)
Real or paid customer showing results. Named user, specific numbers. Works when social proof is the trust barrier.
Storytelling (ST)
First-person arc. "We used to struggle, then we found Zaapi." Works for longer-consideration purchases.
Educational (ED)
UI walkthrough, how-it-works. Best when the buyer is evaluating features, not pains.

Production

AI
AI-generated talking head + overlays. Fastest to test hypotheses before committing real production budget.
IH (In-house)
Shot by Zaapi team. Controlled brief, known cost.
UG (UGC)
Creator films in their own setting. Organic feel — high trust.
KOL
Hired presenter/influencer. Their style, Zaapi's brief.
MG
Motion graphics / animated. Highest production cost — use only after angle is proven.

Metrics

Hook Rate · higher is better ↑
% of impressions that watched ≥3 sec. The single most important TOF signal — did the creative stop the scroll? High = creative is landing.
Thumb-Stop · higher is better ↑
Sister metric to Hook — did viewers physically stop scrolling? High = strong visual pull.
CPM · lower is better ↓
Cost per 1,000 impressions. Rising CPM = audience saturating OR auction getting competitive. Low = not overpaying for reach.
Frequency · lower for TOF, fine for BOF ↓
How many times each user saw the ad. Above 3 = fatigue zone on TOF. Retargeting (BOF) frequency can be higher by design.
FTI · higher is better ↑
First Time Integrated — registered + connected 1 channel. The conversion that matters. Higher = more pipeline.
CPA · lower is better ↓
Cost per FTI. Compare to plan CAC ($550 TH / $739 SEA / $1,800 ROW). Below plan = efficient, above plan = leak.

AI Verdicts

🛑 KILL
Hook < 15%. Creative doesn't land — new targeting won't save a bad ad. Turn it off.
📈 SCALE
TOF: Hook > 35% + Freq < 2.5. BOF: FTI ≥ 2. Proven winner — pour more budget in.
🎯 NEW TARGETING
Creative works (hook OK) but no conversions on ≥$50 spend. Audience is wrong — test a new persona or segment.
📡 NEW CHANNEL
CPM or CPA up >50% WoW. Audience right, placement getting expensive — test IG Reels, Stories, or turn off Audience Network.
♻️ REFRESH CREATIVE
Frequency > 3 and no FTI. Audience saturated — rotate a fresh edit of the same angle.
👀 WATCH
TOF: borderline hook (25–35%) or hook dropped >10pp WoW. BOF: $10–$50 spend with 0 FTI — inconclusive, not enough budget yet to declare the audience wrong.
⏸ PAUSED
Ad ran historically but zero spend last week. Check lifetime metrics below to decide if worth relaunching.
⏳ NO DATA
Never ran meaningfully (<$10 LW, no lifetime). Wait for data.
✅ CONTINUE
Healthy — no action needed, keep running.
